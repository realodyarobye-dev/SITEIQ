Keys ranked by how much they improve SiteIQ

1. GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY TRANSFORMATIVE

This is the single biggest upgrade. Without it, competitor intelligence is roughly half of what it should be.

What it adds: competitor star ratings, review counts, opening hours and 24-hour status, storefront photos, Google Maps links, websites, phone numbers, price levels, and Street View imagery of your storefront in the report, PDF and PowerPoint.

APIs to enable in Google Cloud: Places API (New), Geocoding API, Street View Static API.

Cost: Google gives a monthly free credit that covers light use. A single location report costs roughly 5–15 cents in API calls. SiteIQ caches every response for a week, so re-opening a report or generating its PDF costs nothing.

Set in Railway as: GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY

2. NYC_APP_TOKEN USEFUL, FREE

What it adds: nothing new by itself — NYC Open Data already works without it. It raises your rate limit so comparing 15 addresses at once does not get throttled.

Where: free signup at data.cityofnewyork.us, create an app token.

Set in Railway as: NYC_APP_TOKEN

3. CONTACT_EMAIL DO THIS

OpenStreetMap's free geocoder asks that apps identify themselves with a real contact address. Set this to your email so SiteIQ stays a good citizen and does not get blocked.

Set in Railway as: CONTACT_EMAIL

Keeping your reports between deploys

Railway wipes the filesystem on every redeploy. To keep saved reports and your calibration data, add a Volume in Railway mounted at /data. SiteIQ detects it automatically. Without it the app still works — you just lose history when you redeploy.
